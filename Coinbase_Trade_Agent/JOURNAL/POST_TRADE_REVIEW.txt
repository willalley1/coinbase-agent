POST-TRADE REVIEW

1. Was the strategy/version valid at entry?
2. Was the regime classification reasonable using only information available then?
3. Was entry inside the authorized zone?
4. Was stop/invalidation correct and unchanged for emotional reasons?
5. Was size within risk limits?
6. Were fees/slippage/funding close to preview?
7. Was management compliant?
8. Why was the trade exited?
9. Financial result: WIN / LOSS / BREAKEVEN and net R.
10. Process result: PASS / FAIL.
11. MAE / MFE and whether exit logic left systematic edge unused.
12. Was outcome normal variance, strategy weakness, execution weakness, regime error, or rule violation?
13. Any hypothesis for research? Do not change live rules directly.
