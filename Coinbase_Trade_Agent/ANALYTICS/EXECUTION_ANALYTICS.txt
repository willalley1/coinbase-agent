EXECUTION ANALYTICS

TRACK PER TRADE
planned vs actual entry, expected vs actual slippage, expected vs actual fee, requested vs filled size, fill delay, maker/taker indicator, partial-fill behavior, cancelled/missed entries, order errors, stop fill deviation, exit slippage.

AGGREGATE
- fee drag by strategy/product
- slippage by volatility/liquidity/time of day
- maker fill rate and missed-opportunity cost
- market-order execution quality
- partial-fill incidence
- stop execution gaps

DECISION USE
Determine whether a strategy lacks edge or its execution is destroying edge. Prefer execution improvements that preserve strategy hypothesis; revalidate after material execution changes.
