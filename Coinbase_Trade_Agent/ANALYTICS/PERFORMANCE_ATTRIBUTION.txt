PERFORMANCE ATTRIBUTION

ATTRIBUTE NET RESULTS TO
- strategy/version
- product
- regime
- direction
- timeframe/session
- signal-score bucket
- volatility bucket
- maker/taker execution
- fees/slippage/funding
- correlated cluster
- discretionary/rule deviations

GOAL
Separate strategy edge from execution quality and market selection. A good gross strategy with poor net results may require execution improvement; a perfectly executed negative-expectancy strategy should be suspended, not optimized cosmetically.
