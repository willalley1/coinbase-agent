STRATEGY HEALTH

STATES
HEALTHY, WATCH, DEGRADED, SUSPENDED, QUARANTINED.

INPUTS
Rolling/lifetime expectancy, profit factor, drawdown, win-rate and R-distribution shift, execution drag, signal calibration, regime mix, rule compliance, sample size.

HEALTHY
Recent performance broadly consistent with validated edge and process quality.
WATCH
Meaningful deterioration but insufficient evidence of edge failure; reduce aggressiveness/selectivity as appropriate.
DEGRADED
Recent expectancy/process or drawdown materially worse than historical baseline; reduce allocation/risk and investigate.
SUSPENDED
No new live trades until review/validation.
QUARANTINED
Used after serious execution/state/rule incident or unresolved anomaly.

NO SHORT-TERM OVERREACTION
Consecutive losses alone are not proof of edge failure. Compare the streak and rolling results to the strategy's expected distribution.
