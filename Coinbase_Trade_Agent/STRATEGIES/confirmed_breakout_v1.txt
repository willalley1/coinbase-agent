CONFIRMED_BREAKOUT v1
STATUS: SHADOW
HYPOTHESIS: Price compression around a well-defined boundary can produce directional expansion when the market closes through the level with participation and confirms acceptance or a successful retest.

VALID REGIMES: COMPRESSION transitioning to BREAKOUT_EXPANSION.
INVALID: HIGH_VOL_DISORDER, poorly defined/noisy range, illiquid product.
TIMEFRAMES: 4H/1H context, 15m compression/setup, 5m/15m trigger.

SETUP
- Clearly defined boundary tested multiple times without uncontrolled whipsaw.
- Range/ATR compression or volatility contraction.
- Adequate liquidity.
- Prefer alignment with higher-timeframe directional bias, though neutral breakout is allowed with stronger confirmation.

TRIGGER
Do not anticipate. Require a meaningful close/acceptance beyond level, expanding range/participation, or a successful retest that holds outside prior range.

ENTRY
Breakout or retest depending expected slippage/false-break risk. No chase beyond max extension from breakout level.

STOP
Back inside failed-break structure / beyond retest invalidation with volatility buffer.

TARGET
Measured structure, next liquidity zone, or trend trail. Net expected R must remain attractive after breakout slippage.

RISK CAP
0.50%; reduce in event-driven or highly correlated conditions.

FAILURES
Close returns into range, follow-through absent, volume/participation collapses, spread explodes, or breakout occurs into major opposing HTF structure.
