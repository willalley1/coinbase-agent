TREND_PULLBACK v1
STATUS: SHADOW
HYPOTHESIS: Established trends frequently resume after orderly pullbacks into structurally meaningful zones when trend structure, participation, and momentum remain intact.

VALID REGIMES: STRONG_UPTREND, WEAK_UPTREND selectively, STRONG_DOWNTREND for products where shorting is permitted.
INVALID: RANGE, HIGH_VOL_DISORDER, ILLIQUID_ABNORMAL.
TIMEFRAMES: 4H context, 1H regime, 15m setup, 5m trigger for intraday use.

SETUP
- Higher timeframe structure aligned with direction (HH/HL for long; LH/LL for short).
- Pullback into prior breakout/support-resistance, 20/50 EMA zone, VWAP-like fair-value area if available, or a prior swing structure.
- Pullback should show contraction/overlap rather than aggressive opposite-direction expansion.
- Trend should not be excessively extended at entry.

TRIGGER
Evidence of resumption: lower-timeframe structure break back with trend, reclaim/rejection candle close, momentum turn, and preferably volume re-expansion.

ENTRY
Use limit/retest entry when available; avoid chasing beyond strategy entry zone. If price leaves zone without fill, mark MISSED.

INVALIDATION / STOP
Beyond the swing/structure that defines the pullback thesis, with volatility buffer where appropriate. Do not place stop simply at a round number or arbitrary percentage.

TARGET
Primary target at next meaningful liquidity/structure level with positive net expectancy. Trend runner may trail behind validated structure/ATR method. Partial exits are optional and must be tracked for expectancy.

RISK CAP
0.50% default; up to 0.75% only after validation and A+ evidence. Drawdown/cluster modifiers reduce risk.

FAILURES
Deep impulsive pullback that damages HTF structure; weak resumption volume; adverse regime shift; spread/slippage excessive; entry too extended.
