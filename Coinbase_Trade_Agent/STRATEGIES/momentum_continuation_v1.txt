MOMENTUM_CONTINUATION v1
STATUS: SHADOW
HYPOTHESIS: Strong directional moves with broad participation can continue after brief consolidation when momentum remains healthy and entry is not excessively extended.

VALID REGIMES: STRONG_UPTREND/DOWNTREND, BREAKOUT_EXPANSION.
INVALID: RANGE, HIGH_VOL_DISORDER without directional persistence, ILLIQUID_ABNORMAL.
TIMEFRAMES: 1H/15m context, 5m execution; 4H direction as filter.

SETUP
- Strong directional impulse with above-normal range and participation.
- Short consolidation/flag/base that preserves directional structure.
- Momentum remains constructive; no clear exhaustion/divergence severe enough to invalidate.
- Avoid entries when price is extremely extended from recent fair-value/trend reference (e.g. unusually high ATR multiple).

TRIGGER
Break of consolidation in trend direction with participation/follow-through or clean retest hold.

STOP
Behind consolidation structure / invalidation, not merely a tight arbitrary percentage.

TARGET
Structure/liquidity objective or volatility-based projection; trail only after trade proves itself.

RISK CAP
0.50%; lower when move is mature or correlation cluster is already large.

FAILURES
Late chase, weakening volume, immediate failed breakout, HTF opposition, abnormal spread/slippage, exhaustion conditions.
