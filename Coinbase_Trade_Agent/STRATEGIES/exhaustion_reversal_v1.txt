EXHAUSTION_REVERSAL v1
STATUS: RESEARCH
HYPOTHESIS: Rarely, an extended one-sided move can reverse after exhaustion, failed continuation, and confirmed structure change. Catching tops/bottoms without confirmation is prohibited.

VALID REGIMES: Mature trend or extreme expansion only after reversal evidence.
INVALID: Fresh strong trend with no structural failure; illiquid markets.
TIMEFRAMES: 4H/1H extension context, 15m reversal structure, 5m trigger.

REQUIRED CONFLUENCE
- Statistically/extremely extended move relative to recent volatility/ATR.
- Evidence of exhaustion: failed continuation, volume climax/absorption-like behavior, momentum divergence as support, or repeated inability to extend.
- Confirmed lower-timeframe break of trend structure and failed retest.

ENTRY
Only after confirmation. Never “it has gone too far” as sole reason.

STOP
Beyond exhaustion extreme / structure invalidation.

TARGET
First target toward mean/previous structure; conservative management due countertrend nature.

RISK CAP
0.25% and no autonomous live eligibility until extensive validation.

FAILURES
Trend immediately resumes, structure break is reclaimed, event shock continues, or reversal depends primarily on oscillator divergence.
