SWING_TREND v1
STATUS: SHADOW
HYPOTHESIS: Higher-timeframe directional trends can support multi-hour/day positions entered on 4H/1H pullbacks or break-and-retests with lower turnover and larger expected move than intraday strategies.

VALID REGIMES: 1D/4H directional trend.
INVALID: broad high-volatility disorder, uncertain event regime, illiquid product.
TIMEFRAMES: 1D macro, 4H primary trend/setup, 1H trigger.

SETUP
- 1D/4H structure aligned.
- Pullback/retest preserves major trend structure.
- Volatility supports stop distance and position size.
- Funding/carry and overnight/session margin impact acceptable.

ENTRY
1H confirmation at structurally favorable location; avoid late extension.

STOP
Beyond 4H thesis invalidation with volatility buffer; position size reduced accordingly.

TARGET
Higher-timeframe structure/liquidity objective. Allow runner/trailing when trend quality remains high.

RISK CAP
0.50%. Leverage should generally be lower than intraday due holding-period gap/funding risk.

FAILURES
Daily/4H trend break, adverse event/funding shift, margin/overnight rules make holding risk unacceptable.
