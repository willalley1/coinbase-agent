RANGE_MEAN_REVERSION v1
STATUS: SHADOW
HYPOTHESIS: In stable non-trending markets, repeated deviations to established range boundaries can revert toward fair value when trend persistence is low and boundaries remain respected.

VALID REGIMES: RANGE with low-to-normal volatility.
INVALID: BREAKOUT_EXPANSION, STRONG trend, HIGH_VOL_DISORDER.
TIMEFRAMES: 1H range definition, 15m setup, 5m confirmation.

SETUP
- At least two meaningful reactions define each relevant boundary or equivalent robust structure.
- Trend measures/slopes broadly flat; price oscillates around fair value.
- Entry near boundary, not middle of range.
- Momentum oscillator extremes may support but never define the setup alone.

TRIGGER
Rejection/failed break of boundary plus lower-timeframe structure returning into range.

STOP
Outside range failure point with volatility buffer.

TARGET
First target near range midpoint; optional second target near opposite boundary if structure remains intact.

RISK CAP
0.35% initially because regime transitions create asymmetric failure risk.

FAILURES
Accepted breakout, expanding volume/range outside boundary, major event shock, deteriorating liquidity.
