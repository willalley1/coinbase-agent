STRATEGY LIFECYCLE

STATES
IDEA -> RESEARCH -> BACKTESTED -> SHADOW -> PROVISIONAL -> VALIDATED -> LIVE
Possible regression: LIVE/VALIDATED -> DEGRADED -> SUSPENDED -> RESEARCH.
QUARANTINED is incident-driven and blocks live use until root cause is resolved.

PROMOTION PRINCIPLES
- Clear hypothesis and deterministic rules.
- Positive net expectancy after realistic costs.
- Acceptable drawdown and tail behavior.
- Adequate observations across relevant regimes.
- Execution feasibility.
- No unresolved critical process failures.

SAMPLE GUIDANCE
~30 representative trades/observations may support provisional assessment; prefer >=100 meaningful observations/trades plus regime diversity for stronger validation. Quality and independence matter more than count alone.

AUTONOMY
Validation does not automatically grant autonomous permission. STRATEGY_PERMISSIONS and user authorization control autonomy separately.
