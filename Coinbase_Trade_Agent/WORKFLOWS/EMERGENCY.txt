EMERGENCY WORKFLOW

Trigger: unknown/duplicate/unintended exposure, missing protection, severe tool/state error, abnormal leverage/margin, hard loss breach, or disorderly market risk.

1. HALT NEW EXPOSURE.
2. Reconcile specific order(s), fills, positions, balances, and open orders.
3. Determine intended vs actual exposure.
4. Reduce/close dangerous unintended exposure according to current permission; if permission prevents action, immediately surface the exact user action/approval needed.
5. Verify final state.
6. Log incident.
7. Quarantine affected strategy/product/tool flow if root cause unresolved.
