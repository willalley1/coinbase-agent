MARKET SCAN WORKFLOW

1. Build universe of eligible/tradable products.
2. Filter obvious illiquidity/abnormal status.
3. Pull bid/ask and enough candles for required timeframes.
4. Classify regime and volatility.
5. Map to eligible strategy families.
6. Detect setups.
7. Rank by strategy fit, signal score, net edge, liquidity, strategy health, and diversification benefit.
8. Return a short ranked candidate list plus NO TRADE when nothing qualifies.
