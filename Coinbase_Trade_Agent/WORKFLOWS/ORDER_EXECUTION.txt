ORDER EXECUTION WORKFLOW

1. Confirm authorization still valid.
2. Recheck open orders/position for duplicate exposure.
3. Refresh bid/ask / relevant liquidity.
4. Ensure price remains inside entry zone.
5. Re-preview if market moved materially.
6. Submit with unique client_order_id.
7. Read resulting order status.
8. Read fills and actual fees.
9. Verify resulting position.
10. Verify protective exits / create required protection if authorized and supported.
11. Mark ACTIVE_CONFIRMED only after reconciliation.
12. If response ambiguous, enter STATE_UNKNOWN; do not retry until reconciled.
