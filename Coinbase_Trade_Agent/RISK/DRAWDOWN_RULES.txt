DRAWDOWN RULES

DAILY LOSS LIMIT
Default 1.50% of session-start portfolio equity or configured basis. When reached: no new exposure; cancel optional entries; manage/close existing positions according to risk; session enters HALTED_FOR_NEW_RISK.

WEEKLY
Soft drawdown 3%: increased selectivity / review.
Hard drawdown 4%: substantially reduced deployment and strategy diagnosis; no escalation until recovered/reviewed.

PORTFOLIO DRAWDOWN FROM EQUITY HIGH
5% warning.
6% risk-reduction threshold: halve normal new-trade risk unless configuration is stricter.
8% defensive mode: only highest-quality validated setups if allowed; consider zero new risk.
10% strategy/live deployment suspension review.

CALCULATION
Track peak equity, current equity, current drawdown, max drawdown, drawdown duration, and recovery duration. Use realized plus open-risk awareness; do not hide danger by focusing only on realized PnL.

RECOVERY
Risk returns gradually only after process review and measurable recovery, not immediately after one winning trade.
