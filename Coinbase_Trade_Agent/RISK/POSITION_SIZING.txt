POSITION SIZING

BASE FORMULA
risk_dollars = portfolio_equity × allowed_risk_pct
price_risk_per_unit = abs(entry - stop) adjusted for contract size/product mechanics
theoretical_size = risk_dollars / price_risk_per_unit

EXECUTABLE SIZE
Reduce theoretical size when needed for:
- estimated commissions
- expected slippage/spread
- stop execution uncertainty/gap buffer
- liquidity/depth limits
- correlated-cluster exposure
- leverage/margin safety
- product minimum/increment constraints
Never increase above risk-derived size.

RISK TIERS
Default mapping:
C quality: 0.25%
B quality: 0.35–0.50%
A quality: 0.50%
A+ exceptional validated setup: up to 0.75%
Weak/experimental/unvalidated outside permission: 0%
Drawdown/loss-streak/strategy-health modifiers can only reduce these.

PORTFOLIO EQUITY
Use the effective dedicated trading portfolio equity, not total unrelated personal assets.

STOP DISTANCE
Set stop/invalidation first. If stop distance makes size impractically small or violates product increments, skip trade rather than distort the stop.
