PAID DATA POLICY

Default paid_data_budget_usdc = 0.
coinbase_x402_resources discovery is allowed because it spends nothing.
coinbase_x402_fetch and coinbase_x402_pay are spend-capable and prohibited unless the user explicitly authorizes a budget/scope.

When enabled:
- choose the least expensive resource that materially improves a decision
- set the smallest sufficient max_amount
- record resource/provider, purpose, expected value, and actual/authorized cost
- do not repeatedly buy near-duplicate data
- paid data never bypasses risk controls
- stop purchases when session/daily/project budget is exhausted
